# Quadcopter: Definition, Function, and History

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadcopter

## Definition and Basic Function

A quadcopter is a type of helicopter or multicopter that has four rotors. The small size and low inertia of modern drones make them practical for flight control systems that would be impractical for larger aircraft.

## Design Principles

### Rotor Configuration and Control

Quadcopters typically have two rotors spinning clockwise and two counterclockwise. Flight control works through independent speed variation of each rotor. Pitch and roll are controlled by varying the net centre of thrust, with yaw controlled by varying the net torque.

### Torque Management

When all four rotors spin at equal angular velocity with opposing directions, the net torque cancels out. This eliminates the need for a tail rotor found on conventional helicopters. Yaw control is achieved by offsetting the cumulative thrust commands between the counter-rotating blade pairs.

### Mechanical Structure

The main components include a fuselage or frame, four rotors (fixed or variable-pitch), and motors positioned equidistant for optimal performance.

## Historical Development

### Early Pioneers (1908–1920s)

Louis Breguet designed the first heavier-than-air vertical-takeoff device with four rotors in 1908, though it only flew while tethered. Étienne Oehmichen's 1924 design established the first-ever FAI distance record for helicopters of 360 m.

### Postwar Era (1950s–1960s)

The Convertawings Model A (1956) became the first four-rotor helicopter to demonstrate successful forward flight. However, commercial development stalled due to lack of demand.

### Modern Era (2000s–Present)

The quadcopter configuration gained popularity with advances in electronics around 2005–2010. Small camera-equipped quadcopters became commercially viable, leading to widespread use in recreational drones, commercial applications, and military operations. Recent records show quadcopters reaching speeds exceeding 650 km/h and flight times over 2.5 hours.

## Applications

### Commercial and Recreational

Quadcopters are popular for aerial photography, amateur projects, and autonomous research due to their mechanical simplicity and reliability.

### Military and Security

During the 2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine, quadcopter drones were used extensively for reconnaissance and as small weapons systems, described as a "game changer."

### Criminal Activity

Drug cartels have employed quadcopters to smuggle contraband across borders and into prisons worldwide.

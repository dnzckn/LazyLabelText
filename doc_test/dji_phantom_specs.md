# DJI Phantom Series — Specifications & Component Lists

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DJI_Phantom

Per-model component breakdown for the DJI Phantom quadcopter family. Functions as a bill-of-materials / spec reference across models.

## Overview

The DJI Phantom is a quadcopter UAV series developed by Chinese company DJI, starting with the original release in January 2013. Described as the first commercially successful recreational drone, it revolutionized consumer aerial photography through accessibility and user-friendly design.

## Phantom 1 (2013)

- Battery: 2200 mAh LiPo
- Flight time: ~15 minutes
- Control frequency: 2.4 GHz
- Camera: Optional GoPro HERO mount
- Autopilot: GPS-enabled NAZA-M system

## Phantom FC40

- Control frequency: 5.8 GHz (allowing 2.4 GHz for FPV)
- Camera: Built-in FC40, 720p at 30 FPS
- Control: iOS/Android app via Wi-Fi

## Phantom 2 Vision (2013)

- Battery: 5200 mAh
- Flight time: ~25 minutes
- Camera: Built-in, 1080p at 30/60 FPS, 14MP stills
- Gimbal: One-axis, 60° vertical motion
- Storage: MicroSD card

## Phantom 2 Vision+ (2014)

- Gimbal: Three-axis self-stabilizing, 90° vertical tilt
- Flight time: ~25 minutes
- Key feature: Camera can point straight down

## Phantom 3 Professional (2015)

- Battery: 4480 mAh
- Flight time: ~23 minutes
- Camera: Sony Exmor 1/2.3", 4K at 30 FPS
- Navigation: GPS + GLONASS
- Transmission: Lightbridge (5 km range)
- New feature: Visual positioning system (no-GPS hovering)

## Phantom 3 Advanced (2015)

- Camera: Sony Exmor 1/2.3", 2.7K at 30 FPS (vs Pro's 4K)
- Battery & flight time: Same as Professional
- Transmission: Lightbridge

## Phantom 3 Standard (2015)

- Transmission: Wi-Fi (vs Lightbridge on Pro/Advanced)
- Camera: 2.7K and 1080p at 30 FPS; 720p at 60 FPS
- Missing: Visual positioning system, GLONASS

## Phantom 3 4K (2016)

- Camera: 4K Sony Exmor (like Pro)
- Transmission: Wi-Fi
- Range: 1.2 km (480p live video)

## Phantom 3 SE (2017)

- Battery: 4480 mAh
- Flight time: ~25 minutes
- Video transmission: 720p live (upgraded from 4K's 480p)
- Range: 4 km

## Phantom 4 (2016)

- Battery: 5350 mAh
- Flight time: ~28 minutes
- Camera: 1/2.3", 4K at 30 FPS; 1080p at 120 FPS
- Electronic shutter: Yes
- Obstacle avoidance: 4 cameras (2 forward, 2 downward) + ultrasonic
- New features: ActiveTrack, TapFly
- Propellers: Push-and-release locking mechanism

## Phantom 4 Pro (2016)

- Battery: 5870 mAh
- Flight time: ~30 minutes
- Camera: 20MP Sony Exmor R, 1" CMOS sensor
- Obstacle avoidance: Upgraded with rear-facing and infrared side sensors
- Propellers: Model 9450S

## Phantom 4 Pro+ (2016)

- Identical to Pro except: Controller includes 5.5" built-in screen and integrated flight app

## Phantom 4 Advanced (2017)

- Camera: 1" CMOS with mechanical shutter
- Video: 4K H.264 at 60 FPS; H.265 at 30 FPS

## Phantom 4 Pro V2.0 (2018)

- Camera: 20MP 1" Exmor R
- Transmission: OcuSync
- ESCs: Improved
- Propellers: Model 9455S low-noise (backwards compatible)
- Battery: 5870 mAh / ~30 minutes flight

## Phantom 4 RTK (2018)

- Purpose: Aerial mapping and surveying
- Camera: FC6310R (Exmor R equivalent with glass lens)
- GNSS: Real-time kinematic module; compatible with GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou
- Redundancy: Dual GNSS module
- Recording: JPEG images, MOV video format

## P4 Multispectral (2019)

- Purpose: Agricultural applications
- Camera: Six-sensor array (RGB + 5 narrow-band)
- Battery: 5870 mAh
- Flight time: ~27 minutes
- Propellers: Model 9455S standard

## Transmission Systems Evolution

- Phantom 1–2: Wi-Fi or 2.4/5.8 GHz direct
- Phantom 3 Pro/Advanced: Lightbridge (up to 5 km)
- Phantom 4 Pro V2.0 onward: OcuSync
